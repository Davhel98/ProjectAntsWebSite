About the Game

Project Ants is a 3D Metroidvania that combines the exploration depth of Hollow Knight with the technical hack-and-slash combat of Ninja Gaiden.

Set in a fractured, interconnected insect kingdom, it follows a fallen Phragmotic Ant Warrior exploring a brutal and layered world, uncovering the terrible secrets hidden at its core.

A precision-driven combat experience in a 3D interconnected world, where traversal mastery is as critical as combat skill.
